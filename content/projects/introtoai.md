## Overview

My implementations of the search algorithms covered in an introductory AI course, written from scratch in Python.

## What's inside

- **Best-first search:** a vacuum-cleaner world where an agent moves between clean and dirty cells. States track the agent's position and every cell's condition, and the search looks for a sequence of actions that leaves every cell clean.

More algorithms, such as breadth-first and depth-first search, are planned for the same set of problems.
